# Branding / name-availability notes

_Preliminary search 2026-07-02. NOT legal advice — do a formal USPTO/EUIPO/UK-IPO
clearance + App Store name check before any public App Store listing._

## Finding: "Grimoire" (bare) is heavily crowded in this exact niche

The name conflicts most where it matters — TTRPG campaign management:

- **ttrpg.bot** — "Grimoire | Campaign Manager for D&D & TTRPG GMs" (direct competitor, same name, same category)
- **dungeongrimoire.com** — "Grimoire — D&D Campaign Manager for Dungeon Masters" (direct competitor)
- **hunter-read/grimoire** — self-hosted TTRPG content/campaign organizer
- App Store already carries **"Grimoire Scrolls"** (id6760601517), **"The Grand Grimoire"** (id1503204164), **"Grimoire Incremental"** (Play)
- **GRIMOIRE** — award-winning Australian solo RPG

Shipping as bare **"Grimoire"** would collide with at least two live products literally
named "Grimoire" doing campaign management → trademark risk + poor App Store/SEO
discoverability (buried under existing apps).

## Point in favour: "The Grimoire Archive" is more defensible

The fuller brand **"The Grimoire Archive"** did NOT surface as taken and is more
distinctive than bare "Grimoire". If keeping the Grimoire root, lead with the full
brand everywhere (App Store name included), not just "Grimoire".

## Tagline caution

"Write Your Story. Live Your Legend." — **"Live Your Legend"** has prior use as a
personal-development brand. Not confirmed as a registered mark in this quick pass,
but check USPTO before committing it to the store listing.

## Recommendation

Holding name is fine for TestFlight. Before the **public App Store listing**:
1. Formal trademark clearance for the chosen name (USPTO word-mark search, class 9
   software / class 41).
2. App Store name-availability check (exact + close variants).
3. Either commit to **"The Grimoire Archive"** (file it) or pick a more distinctive
   name; clear the tagline too.
